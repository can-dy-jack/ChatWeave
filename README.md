# Chatweave 涌言
> Your All-in-One AI Chat & Creation Hub in mobile.

[【中文】](./REAME_ZH.md)

Switch between multiple AI models, get real-time streaming responses, and personalize your workspace.

Chatweave is built for people who rely on AI every day.
With Chatweave, you can:
- Connect multiple AI models in one app and switch instantly by task.
- Create and manage reusable agent setups and prompts.
- View responses in real time with streaming output.
- Personalize language, themes, and interface preferences for comfort and focus.
Whether you are a student, creator, developer, or professional, Chatweave becomes your daily AI workspace.
